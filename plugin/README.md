# Newtation AI SEO

Find out whether AI assistants and AI search engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot) can find, read and cite your website, and fix what's stopping them.

## Use it

Ask Claude about AI SEO in plain words. For example:

- "Run an AI SEO audit on example.com"
- "Why doesn't ChatGPT mention my brand?"
- "Can GPTBot and ClaudeBot crawl my site?"
- "What does AI think about Acme? Is it making things up?"
- "Why does Perplexity recommend my competitor instead of me?"
- "Make me an llms.txt file and schema markup for example.com"
- "How often does AI recommend us versus HubSpot for 'CRM for agencies'?"
- "Rewrite our pricing page so AI answers quote it"

Claude checks your robots.txt and firewall against 18 AI crawlers, audits pages across your whole site, reads them the way AI crawlers do (without JavaScript), scores how quotable each section is, checks whether AI recognizes your brand (structured data, Wikidata, Wikipedia) and whether you're in AI training data (Common Crawl), and measures how often AI recommends you versus competitors. You get a score, a ranked fix list, and paste-ready code: robots.txt lines, JSON-LD schema markup and an llms.txt file, plus a checklist you can forward to your developer.

## What's included

- **Skills:** AI visibility audit, AI share of voice, content optimization for AI citations, AI brand perception and hallucination test, and competitor gap analysis
- **Connector:** the Newtation MCP server at `https://mcp.newtation.ai/mcp` — 17 read-only tools, no sign-in needed

## Data

The connector fetches the public web pages you ask about (plus their robots.txt, sitemap and llms.txt), and looks up the brand in the public Wikidata and Common Crawl indexes. Share-of-voice analysis runs on the answers you or Claude provide. It doesn't need an account, doesn't change anything on your site, and doesn't store your conversations.
